Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

📧 Contact Human Support

How do I book tickets on EventSphere?

Simply create a free account, browse our events page, select your preferred event, and choose the number of tickets. Click "Book Now" to confirm your registration instantly.

Can I cancel my booking later?

Yes! You can cancel your booking anytime from the "My Bookings" page. Your seats will instantly be made available for others.

How do I get my tickets?

Once your booking is confirmed, your digital ticket is safely stored in the "My Bookings" section. You do not need to print it; simply show it on your phone at the venue.

Are there any hidden charges?

No, absolute transparency is our priority. The price you see on the event details page is the final price you pay.
